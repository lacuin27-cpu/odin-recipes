Sisig Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1/2 kilo pork belly, boiled and chopped
  • 1/2 kilo pork ears, boiled and chopped
  • 1/2 kilo pork liver, boiled and chopped
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2-3 pieces of chili peppers, chopped
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup vinegar
  • 1/4 cup calamansi juice or lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 egg (optional)

Instructions:

  1. In a pan, sauté garlic and onion until fragrant.
  2. Add the chopped pork belly, ears, and liver. Cook until slightly crispy.
  3. Add the soy sauce, vinegar, and calamansi juice. Stir well and let it simmer for a few minutes.
  4. Add the chopped chili peppers and season with salt and pepper to taste. Cook for another 2-3 minutes.
  5. If desired, crack an egg into the pan and let it cook until done.
  6. Serve hot with steamed rice.
