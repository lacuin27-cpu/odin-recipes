Sinigang Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1/2 kilo pork ribs or pork belly, cut into pieces

Instructions:

  1. In a pot, add the pork pieces and water. Bring to a boil.
  2. Add the vegetables and cook until tender.
  3. Add the tamarind soup base and season with salt and pepper to taste.
  4. Simmer for another 10-15 minutes.
  5. Serve hot with steamed rice.
